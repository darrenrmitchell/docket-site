Privacy Notice

In short: we collect only what we need to contact you about joining Docket. Some of it (the health conditions you tick) is sensitive personal data under Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020. We never sell it. You can ask us to see it, correct it or delete it at any time by writing to mydockethealth@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Docket Technologies Limited ("Docket", "we") is the data controller for the information on this page. We are registered with the Office of the Information Commissioner of Jamaica. Contact: mydockethealth@gmail.com.

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time; we will then delete your entry unless the law requires us to keep it.

5. How long we keep it

Form entries are kept for 12 months from sign-up, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. If you join Docket, the service notice applies from then on.

6. How we use AI

Docket's small team uses AI assistants (Claude, made by Anthropic) to help with operations: for example, preparing internal daily reports and drafting messages for a person on our team to review.

  • A person decides. AI does not decide whether you can join, and it does not make any decision about you or anyone's care.
  • AI does not contact you. Every message you receive from Docket is sent or approved by a person on our team.
  • Minimum data. We give AI tools only the information a task needs, under business terms where Anthropic does not use it to train its models.
  • You can opt out. Write to us and we will exclude your information from AI-assisted processing. This won't change how we treat you.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2020 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, stop or object to its processing, withdraw consent, and have it deleted. Write to mydockethealth@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Information Commissioner of Jamaica.

8. Security and breaches

Access to form entries is limited to named Docket staff. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the Information Commissioner and you, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

9. Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the version and date above. We'll email you before any change in how we use the health information you gave us.
